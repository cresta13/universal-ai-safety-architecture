World impact
Permission is not intelligence. UAIS separates what a system can do from what it may exercise.
Claims need replayable evidence. Verifier-friendly records constrain gates before authority expands.
Human sovereignty stays outside the model path. Independent override, isolation, and recovery remain mandatory where consequences rise.
