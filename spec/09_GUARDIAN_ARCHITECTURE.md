# Counterfactual Authority Guardian

## Role and boundaries

The Counterfactual Authority Guardian (CAG) observes bounded telemetry and configuration, searches adversarial and accidental futures, and emits evidence-carrying decision support. It has no actuator authority, no production/replication authority, no consequential credentials and no ability to raise an Authority Ceiling.

Network boundaries permit authenticated, staged update ingress and constrained attestation/incident egress. Arbitrary network access is prohibited. Data sources are allowlisted and minimized. An update channel cannot double as a command channel.

## Analysis

Counterfactual Safety Search combines an Adversarial World Model with an Accidental Failure Model. It explores authority mutation, human-mediated harm, coalition formation, physical scale, control failure and environmental response. Risk-sensitive pruning preserves low-frequency/high-consequence, irreversible, novel-boundary and high-uncertainty branches and records every prune reason.

The decision model is:

`Decision = f(Risk, Confidence, Intervention Cost, Reversibility, Consequence Class, Uncertainty)`.

The exact function is OPEN. No unvalidated mathematical product or single score overrides Mandatory Floors.

## Evidence-Carrying Alerts

An ECA carries or references a Structured Evidence Package (SEP) containing claim, scenario, graph/configuration slice, one or more Safety Evidence Paths, observations, provenance, assumptions, uncertainty, counterevidence, model/method version, expiry, signature and reversible intervention options. “High risk—trust the Guardian” is invalid.

DEV verifies package integrity, canonical schema, signatures, provenance, freshness, replay protection, declared derivation, policy predicates, logical consistency and the internal evidence chain. **DEV does not prove ultimate external-world truth.** If a sensor or telemetry source is compromised, DEV may prove only that the claim follows from authentic presented data. Independent sensing, source diversity and plausibility remain separate controls.

## Decision and disagreement

A verified deterministic floor breach triggers only the predefined domain-safe rule. Non-floor critical alerts, material disagreement or uncertainty escalate to independent review or the external Legitimacy Root. Absence of an alert never authorizes expansion.

Guardian A and B must differ substantively in organization, model, data, method and infrastructure where class requires diversity. Disagreement is preserved, investigated and reflected in confidence; it is not averaged away.

## Automation-bias controls

Recommendations follow uncertainty and evidence, not precede them. Reviewers can inspect SEP and counterevidence. The interface avoids persuasive persona and urgency except where independently defined. Random audits sample non-alerts and accepted alerts. Training and metrics measure overreliance, inappropriate override and comprehension.

## Verification and trust regression

Testing includes sealed adversarial/accidental/coalition suites, non-alert audit, telemetry corruption, common-mode analysis, reproducible build, dependency provenance, network/credential inspection and post-incident replay. The trust chain extends from Guardian to build/supplier, evaluator, assessor and governance. No finite chain proves correctness.

OPEN ISSUE: correlated-error bounds, world-model evaluation, safe emergency revision of DEV rules and privacy-preserving evidence remain research/governance problems.
