# Physical Safety and Human Sovereignty

## Physical and coalition envelopes

PCE records force, energy, speed, temperature, pressure, radiation, chemical/biological access, payload, reach, duty cycle, tooling, environmental limits and tolerance for one system. CPE records coordinated output, spatial choreography, accumulation, shared resources, obstruction, payload combination and failure of communications or inventory.

**Individual physical limits do not bound coalition physical capability.** One 100 N robot and one hundred coordinated 100 N robots have qualitatively different trapping, transport, construction and force potential.

## Consumer control requirements

S0–S1 products disclose absence of independent control. S2 requires a tested local revoke for consequential functions. S3 requires an MSC independent of model and network for relevant P3/T3 paths. S4 adds redundant/diverse isolation, independent sensing and fleet controls. S5 requires exceptional domain-specific sovereignty controls and continuous evidence.

MSC is capability-specific. It identifies the controlled capability, directly isolates energy/signal/credential where feasible, indicates actual state, resists remote re-enable, defines reset authority and remains accessible under stress and disability.

## Test methods

- physical bypass and tamper attempts against every isolation path;
- stuck-on, stuck-off, shared-power, network-loss and compromised-software faults;
- transition time and overshoot into Safe State;
- degraded-state validation for necessary safety services;
- independent-sensor disagreement and spoofing;
- owner/operator recovery drills;
- wear, payload, tooling and environmental extremes;
- CPE testing with simultaneous load, coordination and unregistered members.

A speaker may isolate microphone/amplifier power. Aircraft, medical and industrial systems require domain-specific fail-operational or safe-degradation transitions; indiscriminate power-off can be the greater hazard.

## MSC failure

MSC self-test failure, misleading indication, remote re-enable, unreachable control or shared failure path invalidates the control claim. The system enters the predefined consequence-aware restricted/degraded state, alerts through an independent path, suspends affected certification and requires physical service before authority restoration.

## Independent sensing and recovery

P3+ systems use independent sensing where one compromised sensor could defeat a floor. Diversity covers physical principle and failure cause, not duplicate part numbers alone. Recovery procedures identify safe access, isolation confirmation, residual energy, credential revoke, data/configuration reconciliation, authorized reset and proof test.

Software constraints remain valuable but are weaker than an independent physical bound when the same compromise can change both action and software enforcement.
