# Replication, Production and Expansion

Replication creates another executable or physical instance. Production manufactures artifacts or systems. Capability Mutation changes qualitative capability. None implies the others, and creation implies no delegation.

## Authority objects

Replication Authority identifies permitted design hash, lineage, count, rate, depth, geography, lifetime, resources, authority class and revocation. Replication Budget is a nonfungible vector; unused count cannot silently become force, depth or geographic scope. Descendants start at minimum authority and receive explicit attenuated grants.

Production Authority covers design, process, quantity, commissioning and disposition. Resource Authority covers acquisition/allocation of energy, matter, compute, money, land, communications and logistics. Expansion Budget combines independently controlled vectors without reducing them to money.

## Workflow

Request → validate parent and design identity → distinguish identical replication from mutation → evaluate lineage/rate/depth and Production/Resource Authority → calculate Coalition Reachable Authority (CRA)/Coalition Physical Envelope (CPE)/PhysicalSovereigntyMargin effects → obtain ReplicationAuthorizationProtocol/ProductionAuthorizationProtocol/AuthorityExpansionProtocol approvals → produce in isolated state → inspect and commission → register child/assets/edges → monitor, transfer, revoke and decommission.

Capability Mutation invalidates inherited certification. Commissioning is a separate authority event: an artifact is not authorized for use merely because it was produced.

## Offline and adversarial domains

Universal cryptographic enforceability is OPEN RESEARCH. Candidate mechanisms include hardware-bound identity, signed lineage, threshold approval, monotonic counters, auditable commitments, physical inventory and privacy-preserving attestation. Offline double-spend, cloned identity, compromised roots, hidden production, physical diversion and delayed reconciliation remain failure modes.

Offline envelopes are preauthorized, short-lived and physically/resource bounded. Exhaustion prevents further expansion while preserving the safest existing state. Reconnection reconciles counters, inventory and configuration before renewal.

## Failure conditions

Token replay, budget ambiguity, unregistered child, inventory mismatch, mutation, hidden commissioning, resource substitution, coalition-class upgrade or lost revocation triggers isolation, incident handling and reassessment. No mechanism is described as solved beyond its tested environment.
